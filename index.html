OpenITI Book Classifier

ADHFAIS APP{ENDIX} 2: M10 Book Classification, 2025 (OpenITI, Release ver. 2023.1.8) · companion of the OpenITI NgramReader

How to use

  • Type a few letters of a title, an author's name (in transliteration or Arabic) or an OpenITI URI into the search box; matching ignores case and diacritics (“tabari tarikh” finds Taʾrīḫ al-rusul wa-l-mulūk of al-Ṭabarī). Choose a book or an author.
  • A book profile shows the book's typology as a whole (radar) and along the text (rolling assessment), the entropy of its vocabulary along the text and against all books, its nearest books by vocabulary (TF-IDF), and the profile of its author. Section headers can be shown on the rolling plots.
  • An author profile shows the author's typology over all their books and the list of the books.
  • Profiles have their own addresses, ?book=<bookURI> and ?author=<authorURI>; the NgramReader links to them from its tables of books and authors.
  • Every plot and table can be downloaded: the data behind it as TSV (or all data of a profile as a zip), the plots as SVG or PNG (buttons above each plot).

Book titles and author names

Titles and names are shown in transliteration with the Arabic beside the OpenITI URI. Book titles and author names are working labels and may contain errors; the URI is authoritative. The labels were generated with a language model; a raised ? marks a label flagged as uncertain. Without a label the URI is shown.
