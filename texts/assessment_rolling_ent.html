Rolling entropy assessment allows us to see how the complexity of the text varies. Rolling assessment is based on the evaluation of overlapping sections of texts, where each section is evaluated separately. The graph below shows the fluctuations of entropy calculated on unigrams and bigrams.

NB: If you see “No data for this text.” instead of the rolling graph, it means that the rolling assessment is not possible due to the insufficient length of the selected text. (This usually happens with particularly short texts.)
