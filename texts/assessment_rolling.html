Rolling assessment allows us to see which parts of the work have strong signal for particular categories of comparables. Rolling assessment is based on the evaluation of overlapping sections of texts, where each section is evaluated separately. The graph above shows the fluctuations of signals of different categories of comparables: a flat line denotes the complete lack of the signal, while the filled area shows varying degrees of the signal.

NB: If you see “No data for this text.” instead of the rolling graph, it means that the rolling assessment is not possible due to the insufficient length of the selected text. (This usually happens with particularly short texts.)
