This is an experiment on the classification of works from the Arabic Written Tradition as it is represented by the OpenITI Corpus. Each work is measured against a group of texts that have been selected to represent a particular type of texts (“genre”?). With this approach we can avoid “hard” classification where any given work has to be squeezed into a particular category (“genre”) at the expense of being excluded from others. The evaluations are made using M10 model, containing 10 main types.
